Important Limitations

What this model cannot do

SATYA is a research tool, not an oracle. Understanding these limitations is essential for responsible use.

Probabilistic, Not Definitive

Every prediction is a probability estimate. A "Likely Fake" verdict means the model assigns a higher probability to manipulation — it does not constitute proof. Decisions based on these results should always involve human judgment.

False Positives Occur

Authentic images may be incorrectly classified as manipulated. Unusual lighting, heavy compression, artistic filters, or uncommon camera sensors can trigger false positives. The model's test precision is 76.4%, meaning roughly 1 in 4 "fake" predictions may be incorrect.

False Negatives Occur

Sophisticated manipulations may evade detection. The model's test recall is 85.0%, meaning approximately 15% of manipulated images in the test set were not detected. New generation techniques may produce even lower detection rates.

Domain Sensitivity

The model was trained on a specific dataset. Images from significantly different domains — different cameras, resolutions, compression levels, or manipulation techniques — may produce less reliable results than the benchmark metrics suggest.

Not Forensic Evidence

Model output should not be used as legal evidence, for content moderation decisions in isolation, or as the sole basis for accusations. It is a screening tool that should inform — not replace — professional forensic analysis.

Evolving Threat Landscape

Image manipulation technology advances continuously. A model trained today may become less effective against tomorrow's techniques. Regular retraining and evaluation against new datasets is essential for maintaining detection capability.
